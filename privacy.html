Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy covers race-uploader, a personal tool that turns my own race-car camera footage into a combined video and uploads it to my own YouTube channel. It also applies to the other projects listed on this site.

What race-uploader accesses

The tool signs in with Google and asks for permission to manage your YouTube account (the youtube.upload and youtube scopes). It uses that permission only to:

  • upload the video files you point it at, with the title, description and tags you provide;
  • set the video's thumbnail;
  • add the uploaded video to a playlist you name.

It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscriptions or account data.

Where your data goes

  • The tool runs entirely on your own computer. There is no server, database or analytics.
  • Your Google sign-in token is saved only in a local file (token.json) on your computer.
  • Video files and metadata are sent only to YouTube, through Google's official API.
  • Nothing is collected by, shared with, or sold to the developer or any third party.

Data retention and deletion

  • race-uploader does not store any data it receives from YouTube API Services. The only thing it keeps from an upload is the new video's link, written to a text file in your race folder.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept in token.json on your computer until you delete it or revoke access. Deleting that file removes all stored authorization data from your computer.
  • After you revoke access (see below), race-uploader can no longer reach your account, and any saved token stops working.
  • The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. Videos you upload remain on YouTube and are managed or deleted in YouTube Studio.

Google API Services

race-uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or at Google security settings. Deleting token.json also removes the saved sign-in from your computer.

Terms

Use of race-uploader is also covered by its Terms of Service.

Other projects

The apps and games on this site do not collect personal information unless their own page says otherwise.
