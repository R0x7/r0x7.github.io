race-uploader

Turns the footage from several cameras on a race car into one synced video and uploads it to my YouTube channel, with no manual editing.

How it works

  1. Drop in the footageWindshield, in-car and hand-held camera files go into one folder per race.
  2. Sync by soundThe tool lines the cameras up by matching their audio tracks, to within a frame.
  3. Renderffmpeg builds one video: the windshield view full-screen, the in-car camera as an inset, and the hand-held start footage popping in while it was rolling.
  4. UploadThe finished video is uploaded to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel, with its title, description, thumbnail and playlist.

What it does with your YouTube account

race-uploader signs in with Google OAuth and uses the YouTube Data API only to:

  • upload videos (videos.insert);
  • set the uploaded video's thumbnail (thumbnails.set);
  • add the uploaded video to a playlist you choose (playlistItems.insert).

It runs entirely on your own computer. Your sign-in token stays in a local file, and nothing is sent anywhere except YouTube. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
