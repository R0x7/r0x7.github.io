WhistleSong

Whistle a melody into your phone and the app autotunes it, finds the key, then writes the rest of the song around it in a style you pick. Bass, chords, and drums are arranged and rendered by a small built-in synth. Everything runs on-device and offline, with no network and no API keys.

Ularn

Julian Olds' 2004 roguelike, Ularn 1.6.3a, brought to modern devices without rewriting it. The original C game core runs almost unmodified: on Android through the NDK with a Kotlin UI, pinch-to-zoom map, save slots, and fully customizable on-screen controls; in the browser compiled to WebAssembly with a shared leaderboard.

GarminRace

A GPS lap timer for track days and motorsport on the Garmin Venu 2. Start/finish crossings are interpolated between GPS fixes so laps are timed to the hundredth, with a live delta against your record lap, split lines for sectors, and vibration and tone alerts. Sessions save to Garmin Connect as FIT activities with the GPS track, heart rate, and lap data.
