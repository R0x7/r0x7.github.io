WhistleSong

Whistle a melody and the app autotunes it, works out the key, then composes the rest of the song in a style you pick. Everything runs on-device and offline, with no network or API keys.

GarminRace

A GPS lap timer for track days and motorsport, built for the Garmin Venu 2. Start/finish lines are timed to the hundredth by interpolating between GPS fixes, with sectors, live delta to your record lap, and FIT export to Garmin Connect.
